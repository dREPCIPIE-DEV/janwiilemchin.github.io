About

Music for the spaces
between things.

Jan-Willem Chin is an independent musician and producer crafting immersive soundscapes designed to help you unwind. Focusing on lofi beats, soothing piano melodies, and nostalgic textures, his music provides the perfect soundtrack for studying, working, or simply relaxing after a long day.


He consistently drops evocative singles that resonate with the global lofi community, including tracks like Movie Night and his latest release, Move like the water. Immerse yourself in his sonic world and let the stress of everyday life fade away.
